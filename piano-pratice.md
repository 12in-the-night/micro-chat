# Warmup Essentials

1. **D chromatic contrary — hands together** — try two octaves  
   `1312313131231`  
   Practice in staccato, and legato soft to loud.

2. **LH only C major scale, 2 octaves — hands together**  
   `543213214321321`  
   Soft to loud.

3. **C major pentatonic with flat 3 note added (Major blues)**  
   RH only: C D Eb E G A …  
   `123 123 123 123 …`

4. **LH Bassline — 4 notes only: 1–3–4–5**  
   In all 12 keys — apply corresponding major blues.

5. **RH — arpeggios and inversions of all primary chords in all 12 keys**  
   I–IV–V7
